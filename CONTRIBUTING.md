# Contributing

This repository is a daily directory, not a dump of everything on the internet.

- **Submit a repo** with the [issue form](https://github.com/mjmirza/awesome-trending/issues/new?template=submit-repo.yml). Give the URL, pick a niche, and write one line on why it belongs.
- Only public repositories, papers, or posts. No private links.
- Do not open a pull request just to add your repo to a trending table. Those tables are generated from the day's sources.
- Niche lists are seeded from the same day's sources. A gap does not mean the topic is ignored.
- The [Featured in awesome-trending](docs/badge.md) badge is only for repos that actually appeared here.
- Numbers must come from a cited source. Do not invent counts.
- The daily layout stays the same: a 30-second summary at the top, jump links, short tables, and long lists inside `<details>`.
- Contributions are accepted under the [OpenRoots Agent License 2.3](LICENSE) (SPDX `LicenseRef-OpenRoots-ORA-2.3`).
